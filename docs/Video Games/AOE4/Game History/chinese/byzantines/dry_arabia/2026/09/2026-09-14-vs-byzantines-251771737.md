## Opening Strategy
Went for a somewhat fast castle instead of the usual aggressive feudal play against Byzantines. Resource allocation wasn't ideal, but transitioned into a turbo Knight + Archer composition with about 3 Nests of Bees for siege pressure.

## Opponent Strategy
Very defensive Byzantines — towered berries and gold immediately. Turtled up and played for the late game with a heavy longbow and spear composition.

## What Went Well
- The fast castle pivot was a good read against the defensive style
- Even though the initial Imperial fight was bad (running into too many spears), unit reinforcements turned it around
- After killing the opponent's longbows in a clean fight, the transition to handcannoneers sealed the game
- Adaptation mid-game was strong — recovering from a rough fight into a winning position

## What Went Wrong
- Resource allocation during the fast castle was suboptimal
- Took a bad fight into too many spears with the upgraded army — should have been more patient
- Lacked defensive infrastructure (Great Wall Gate landmark or keep) to cover the front of the base, which would have avoided the risky engagement

## What Should Be Done In The Future
- When going fast castle, nail the resource allocation — don't let the eco slip
- Against defensive Byz, don't rush into fights with spear-heavy armies; use defensive landmarks (Great Wall Gate) or keeps to secure the base and pick better engagements
- Having a keep or landmark covering the front allows you to fight on your terms instead of being forced into bad trades
